# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People connecting ChatGPT to their own computer and managing local agent resources.

## Product Purpose

Make it straightforward to configure tunnels, browse and install skills, and manage agent profiles and MCP servers from a local web app.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, and practical. Follow the existing OpenAI Platform-inspired dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid distracting decorative effects and unfamiliar controls.

## Design Principles

- Make common tasks easy to find and complete.
- Show the source and outcome of actions clearly.
- Keep controls and language consistent with the existing dashboard.
- Give errors and empty states useful next steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep text readable and controls usable with a keyboard. Respect reduced motion.
